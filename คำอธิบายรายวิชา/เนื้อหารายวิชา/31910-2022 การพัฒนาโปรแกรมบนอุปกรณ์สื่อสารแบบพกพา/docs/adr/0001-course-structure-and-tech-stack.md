# 1. Course Structure and Technology Stack for Mobile App Development (31910-2022)

## Context
Students in Vocational High-Certificate (ปวส. 1) Digital Business need to develop business-ready mobile applications within 4 hours/week over 15 weeks (60 hours total), targeting practical, zero-friction, production-capable business outcomes without prior complex programming prerequisites.

## Decision
We structure the curriculum into 15 sequential chapters strictly mapped to the 15-week workshop syllabus:
1. **Design Foundation (Chapters 1-4)**: Problem framing with App Concept Canvas, followed by Figma for Wireframing, Hi-Fi UI, and Interactive Mobile Prototyping.
2. **Database & Core App Development (Chapters 5-8)**: Google Sheets as relational database, AppSheet for data modeling, forms, validations, relations (`Ref`), and calculation formulas.
3. **Hardware & Workflow Automation (Chapters 9-12)**: Actions, Barcode/QR scanning, Digital Signature, GPS/Google Maps integration, and Event-driven Automation Bots (Email & PDF receipts).
4. **Capstone & Production Rollout (Chapters 13-15)**: Multi-sprint agile delivery, real-world user testing, and live demo presentation.

## Consequences
- Students achieve tangible deliverables in every single chapter without getting blocked by native mobile SDK toolchain setups.
- Content must provide explicit click-by-click instructions, precise formula syntax, and running business case studies so any beginner can achieve 100% identical outcomes independently.
