# 2. Three Business Archetypes for Project-Based Learning

## Context
Students work in groups of 3 to conceptualize and develop their own unique mobile business applications. To provide actionable guidance without restricting their creativity, instructional materials must offer diverse reference models rather than a single business domain.

## Decision
We adopt three distinct business archetypes across all workshop chapters:
1. **Archetype 1: Retail & Food Ordering (สินค้า/อาหาร/สั่งซื้อ)**: Focuses on product catalog, cart management, quantity calculation, and checkout flow.
2. **Archetype 2: Service & Booking (บริการ/นัดหมาย/จองคิว)**: Focuses on calendar time slots, service selection, customer contact info, and booking status lifecycles.
3. **Archetype 3: Asset & Operations Management (คลังพัสดุ/ยืม-คืน/จัดการทรัพย์สิน)**: Focuses on barcode identification, inventory check-in/out, item conditions, and return dates.

## Consequences
- Every student project will map cleanly to one of these three structural patterns.
- Instructional examples in each chapter demonstrate how to implement the required skill across all 3 archetypes.
